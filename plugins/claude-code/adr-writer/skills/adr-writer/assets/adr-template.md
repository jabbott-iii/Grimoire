## Decision

We will state the decision here in full sentences, in the active voice, specific enough that anyone can tell whether it is being followed. Say where it applies and where it does not.

## Consequences

Describe every consequence of applying the decision.

- **Positive:** what becomes easier, cheaper, safer or possible.
- **Negative:** what becomes harder or more expensive, the risks taken on, and the options given up.
- **Neutral:** other changes people need to know about.
- **Follow-up:** work the decision creates, and when or under what condition to review it.
